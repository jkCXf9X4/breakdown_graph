# Intent — why bdg exists

## Purpose

The engineer or agent deciding what bdg must optimize uses this node as the
intent rung; without it, a tool mechanism is defended as a goal and scope grows
without a failure to justify it.

---

The tool exists because a layered breakdown loses credibility when its layers
are copied instead of linked, and because an agent cannot hold a large tree in
one context. The three claims below are the ends; each item states why it
belongs.

Members, in argument order:

1. (Credibility belongs to the producing chain)[[11-1,breaks_down]] is the chain claim the other two depend on.
2. (Large trees need a bounded agent context)[[12-1,breaks_down]] states the context bound that makes a single view necessary.
3. (Layered breakdowns lose credibility across handovers)[[13-1,breaks_down]] states the handover failure that explicit links and freshness pins correct.

All three are ends. A claim that names a file, a command, or a storage choice
has already moved to Product or Architecture.
