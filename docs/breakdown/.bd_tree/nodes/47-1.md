# Known limits and open questions

## Purpose

The maintainer deciding what a clean check licenses uses this finding to keep
design limits visible; without it, structural acceptance is read as chain
certification.

---

The finding is that bd_tree records a producing chain but does not merge or
certify it, and several design limits bound what the check can close. Versions do
not sync across branches and three-way merge is manual (`docs/DESIGN.md:625-628`);
distance is a graph metric, not a semantic order (`docs/DESIGN.md:631-633`);
symlinks require POSIX (`docs/DESIGN.md:638`); and no CI configuration exists
(`docs/DESIGN.md:642-643`). Seven questions remain contested, including version
immutability, clone/replace coupling, view merging, and shared work areas
(`docs/DESIGN.md:645-661`). This finding assesses
(Credibility belongs to the producing chain)[[11-1, assesses]]: the graph records the chain, but recording and
merging are different acts. Impact: a green check cannot certify cross-branch
synthesis or semantic priority, and the questions stay contested findings rather
than selected investigations.
