# Stale and reconcile are view-scoped

Freshness checks operate on the propagating edges of one containment view, not on the whole store.

- **View selection:** `bd stale` and `bd reconcile` accept `--view REF` and otherwise use the store default root.
- **Report:** `bd stale` lists stale and untracked edges with their source, target, and type.
- **Repair:** `bd reconcile` rewrites pins for the named refs, or for every edge with `--all`, inside that view.
- **Review gate:** reconciliation is an explicit acknowledgement that the new target body is the intended one.
- **Isolation:** an edge outside the selected view is not inspected, so unrelated alternatives do not produce noise.
