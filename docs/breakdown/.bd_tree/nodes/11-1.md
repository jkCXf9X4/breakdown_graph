# Credibility belongs to the producing chain

## Purpose

The verifier deciding whether a bd_tree claim is supported uses this node to
reject model-local evidence; without it, each layer's evidence is accepted
without the chain that produced it.

---

Credibility is a property of the producing chain, not of an isolated node or
model. A result is credible only when the path from intent through architecture,
implementation, execution, and recorded evidence is intact; a passing check at
one layer cannot certify another. This is the failure the tool makes visible:
the graph records the links, and a freshness pin records that the upstream text
still matches. The claim is falsified by a result that remains credible after its
upstream assumptions or execution record are removed.

Source: `docs/DESIGN.md:9-24` states the originating problem and the chain
argument. The tool applies that diagnosis; the claim is the reason the topology
is explicit, not a claim about any one model.
