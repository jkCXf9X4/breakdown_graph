# What does the Summary of Included Papers chapter deliver?

The summary chapter makes each appended paper readable without opening the appendix: four sections in paper-number order, one per paper.

- **Purpose:** give the reader what each paper contributes, its bound, and where it sits in the chain, so the synthesis can cite the summaries.
- **Sections:** four numbered sections, Paper I to Paper IV, in paper-number order (`ARG-003`).
- **Per section:** the objective, what the paper does, its evidence bound, and one chain-role line (`RES-001` to `RES-004`).
- **Claims:** none beyond what the paper nodes establish; a paper's evidence bound must not be exceeded (`CLM-002`).
- **Order:** after Methods, before Synthesis; the chapter itself carries no floats (`MS-014`).
