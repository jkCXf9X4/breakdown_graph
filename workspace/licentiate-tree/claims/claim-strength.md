# How strong is the claim?

The claim's strength boundary: how strong the claim is, read against the alternatives.

- **Magnitude:** weaker than a weakest-link property — the claim locates loss or corruption at named handovers; it does not claim the composed result is numerically robust.
- **Not a minimum rule:** the claim does not aggregate credibility over process steps as the literature's minimum rule does.
