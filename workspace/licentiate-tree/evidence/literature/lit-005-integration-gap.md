# What do the CAS's own authors say about model integration?

The gap claim needs the integration gap stated by the framework's own authors, not only read into it.

- **Finding:** a NESC recommendation accompanying the Credibility Assessment Scale states it "makes no distinction between individual models and integrated models".
- **Recommendation:** assessment "should eventually be refined to account for the additional issues associated with integration of submodels" (`babulaNASAStandardModels2009` L447-451).
- **Status:** a positive statement of the integration gap by the framework's own authors, not a reading of scope.
- **Verification:** quoted from the pinned library's extracted text; surfaced by the 2026-11 review (`IMP-020`).
- **Serves:** the narrowed gap claim (`IMP-020`) and the framework table's integration reading (`MS-003`, `IMP-018`).
