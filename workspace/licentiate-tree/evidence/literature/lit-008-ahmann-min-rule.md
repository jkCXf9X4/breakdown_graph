# How does the simulation-credibility literature aggregate over process steps?

The simulation-credibility literature aggregates credibility over process steps as a minimum, not as a chain-weakener.
- **Minimum rule:** the overall credibility level equals the lowest credibility level of a single process step.
- **Conservative:** the minimum rule takes the weakest step's level as the aggregate, so no step's shortfall is masked.
- **Contrast:** the rule is a per-process-step minimum, distinct from the thesis's located-at-a-handover strength argument (`ARG-009`).
- **Source:** `ahmannContinuousSimulationCredibility2022` lines 810-812, verified in the pinned library's extracted text.
- **Serves:** the compensation and strength positioning (`ARG-009`), and the background chapter (`MS-003`).
