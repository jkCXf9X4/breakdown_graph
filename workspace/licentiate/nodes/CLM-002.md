# What does the licentiate refuse to claim?

The licentiate refuses four claims, each with its bound.
- **No higher numerical accuracy:** the contract changes what can be audited, not the numbers (`ARG-006`).
- **No measured gain:** no effort, defect rate, or productivity figure is claimed.
- **No cross-domain generality:** the mechanism claims rest on the appended papers and the one in-repo case (`INT-002`, `RES-001` to `RES-004`).
- **No new empirical work:** the evidence stops at the appended papers and the in-repo case.

**Owns:**

- The four refusals the licentiate makes.
- The bound that justifies each refusal.
