# What does design research establish as method?

The design-research framing that legitimizes a compilation thesis built from artifacts rather than hypothesis tests.

- **Cycle:** research clarification, a descriptive study of current practice, a prescriptive phase in which artifacts are built, and an evaluation phase (`dregoEarlyProductDevelopment2024`).
- **Evaluation:** artifacts are judged by whether they address the identified problem, not by a hypothesis test.
- **Verification:** cited in the methods chapter; grounded in the pinned library's extracted text.
- **Serves:** the design-research warrant (`ARG-005`), the method record (`MTH-001`), and the methods chapter (`MS-005`).
