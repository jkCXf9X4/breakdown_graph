# What does the Author Contributions chapter deliver?

The author contributions chapter accounts for authorship per appended paper, as the licentiate format requires.

- **Purpose:** satisfy the authorship obligation (`OPS-011`).
- **Content:** co-authors, venue and year per paper; Paper IV's in-press status; the role each author played per paper.
- **Roles:** design, implementation, evaluation or analysis, and writing; no invented percentage is printed (`OPS-011`).
- **Order:** last numbered chapter, before the appended papers (`MS-010`).
- **Artifact anchor:** `thesis/chapters/09_author_contributions.tex#ch:author_contributions`.
