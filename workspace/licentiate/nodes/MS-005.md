# What does the Methods chapter deliver?

The methods chapter reports how the appended evidence was produced and how the kappa's own argument is bounded, so a reader can judge both.

- **Purpose:** report the research method behind the papers (`MTH-001`, `MTH-002`, `MTH-003`) and the methodological argument (`ARG-005`).
- **Sections:** Research design; Research approach; Determinism and reproducibility (operational meaning); Threats to validity and limitations, ending with Threats to the thesis argument in four paragraphs; Artifacts and reproducibility.
- **Research approach:** the design-research framing, the survey reporting rule, the single-case bound and the candidate's structural bias are `MTH-002` and `ARG-005`.
- **Determinism:** the operational definition is `MTH-003`; bounds and artifacts sit in the `tab:evidence-base` and `tab:artifacts` tables, not in the threats prose.
- **Tables:** `tab:evidence-base` states per paper what the evidence establishes and what it does not; `tab:artifacts` names the artifacts; repository locations in its caption must be either cited or stated as the candidate's record (`R-010`).
- **Claims:** none of its own; it reports method and bounds.
- **Literature:** `LIT-006` and `LIT-007` own the reproducibility and design-research facts the chapter reports.
- **Artifact anchor:** `thesis/chapters/05_methods.tex#ch:methods`, `thesis/chapters/05_methods.tex#tab:evidence-base`, `thesis/chapters/05_methods.tex#tab:artifacts`.
