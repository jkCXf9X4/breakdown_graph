# Text obligations

The kappa must state every element below in its own text, so a reader who has not read the papers never has to reconstruct one.

- **Claim:** stated in the wording fixed for it (`CLM-001`).
- **Failure modes:** a handover either carries the information the next step needs, corrupts it, or loses it (`ARG-002`).
- **Strength:** the claim is stated as weaker than a weakest-link property and as the thesis's own construct (`ARG-009`).
- **Refutation:** both refutation conditions are stated (`ARG-009`).
- **Refusals:** the four refusals are stated, each with its bound (`CLM-002`).
- **Delimitation:** the text states that the contract has three clauses (`ARG-006`) and that execution and evaluation lie outside the objective (`INT-002`).
- **Contract:** the three-clause contract, the per-decision scope, the operating conditions travelling with the claim, and the cost must all be stated in the text (`ARG-006`).
- **Open cost:** the text carries the caveat that no paper measures the cost of a wrong judgement (`INT-004`).

**Owns:**

- The obligations on the kappa text that derive from the intent layer.
- Which intent statements must appear in the text.

**Excludes:**

- `CLM-001`, `CLM-002`, `CLM-003`, `ARG-009`, `ARG-006`, `INT-004` hold the statements themselves; `INT-002` and `ARG-002` hold the delimitation and the failure modes.
- `MS-001` to `MS-011` where each statement is printed.
- `ARG-004` how claims are traced to results.
- `VER-007` whether the stated text passes.
