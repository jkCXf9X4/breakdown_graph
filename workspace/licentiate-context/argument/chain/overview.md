# How is the credibility chain organized?

The credibility chain is the organizing model for the kappa: three stages in one order, definition, intended use, evidence, with the Paper I premise as a frame above them.

- **Premise (frame, not a stage):** the premise stated in `INT-001`; Paper I carries it, and it adds no clause. The thesis calls it the motivation gap, which is not a fourth gap.
- **Stage 1 Definition:** what is simulated is hand-derived from the architecture, so the trace must be generated and kept current; Paper IV, closing the `INT-001` definition gap.
- **Stage 2 Intended use:** a result is credible only for a stated use; Paper II, closing the `INT-001` intended-use gap.
- **Stage 3 Evidence:** the workflow that produced a result must be auditable and repeatable; Paper III, closing the `INT-001` evidence gap.

- **Derivation:** one stage per property the primary question demands and an appended paper delivers; a handover with no appended paper is a delimitation, not a stage (`VER-001`).
- **Arithmetic:** four appended papers split as one premise and three answers, and each answer covers exactly one clause of `ARG-006` and claims no other.
- **Synthesis value:** the kappa makes the papers' implicit dependencies explicit instead of re-summarizing them, which is what supports the claim in `CLM-001`.
- **Contract:** the synthesis applies the credibility clauses (`ARG-006`) to the definition layer that precedes them (`ARG-007`); mixing makes that contract costlier to keep valid (`CLM-001`).
- **Provenance split:** provenance separates evidence production (Paper III) from definition (Paper IV).
- **Reuse:** the synthesis argues which metadata and evidence keep credibility under reuse and composition.
- **Trade-offs:** interoperability against overhead, performance against validity and causality, process against numerical credibility.
- **Figure reading:** the definition, intended-use and evidence stages each add one clause to the contract (`ARG-006`), the premise is a frame and not a stage, and the trade-off the figure carries is the cost and complexity of making those clauses explicit and keeping them current.
- **Figure form and placement:** `fig:credibility-chain` is drawn inline as five boxes in one row with a dashed premise frame and a cost arrow; its form and placement are `MS-014`.
