# Which paper plays which role in the chain?

The set is four appended papers, one premise and three answers, as `ARG-001` derives.

- **Enumeration:** the canonical paper enumeration is numeric order I to IV, while the chain runs IV, II, III and the synthesis table lists the premise row for Paper I after Definition (`MS-007`).
- **Paper I — the premise:** characterizes cross-domain M&S practice across the three kinds in scope (`RQ-001`) and how standardized interfaces affect credibility, traceability, and collaboration, framing the three properties without delivering one; it carries no clause.
- **Paper II — the intended-use clause:** makes intended use explicit as machine-interoperable metadata carried alongside the model definition, via operational domain and requirement verification evidence, so reuse outside justified conditions is checked automatically.
- **Paper III — the evidence clause:** provides verification evidence as an auditable and repeatable workflow record for complex V&V, traceable to the workflow, inputs and versions that produced it.
- **Paper IV — the definition clause:** makes the system definition traceable to an authoritative SysML v2 analysis architecture, so the executed composition answers the definition clause.
- **Demonstration split:** only the papers carry clauses; the demonstration runs on the in-repo case (`RES-005`), argued in the synthesis (`MS-007`) and documented in the appendix (`MS-011`).
- **Boundary:** the evidence bound of each paper is `RES-001` to `RES-004`; the thesis-wide refusals are `CLM-002`.

- The premise-versus-answer mapping of the paper set.
- The canonical enumeration and the synthesis ordering.

- `ARG-001` for the stage facts, the derivation rule and the arithmetic; `ARG-002` for the handoff sentences and handoffs by citation.
