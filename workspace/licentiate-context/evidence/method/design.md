# How was the research conducted?

The kappa reports the method behind the appended papers in the Methods chapter (`MS-005`), and this node is the authority for what that method is.

- **Approach:** engineering research in the design-research sense, with problems identified in practice and artifacts evaluated against the problem rather than against a hypothesis.
- **Cycle:** the cycle runs research clarification, a descriptive study of current practice, artifact building, and evaluation.
- **Label:** the kappa claims no stricter label than the papers support (`RES-001` to `RES-004`).
- **Claim:** the method informs the chain argument (`ARG-001`) and the thesis claim (`CLM-001`).
- **Artifact anchor:** `thesis/chapters/05_methods.tex#ch:methods`.

**Owns:**

- The research approach, the design-research cycle, and the label the kappa claims.

**Excludes:**

- `MTH-002` for the survey, case and generality rules; `MTH-003` for the determinism definition.
- `ARG-005` for why the approach is appropriate and how bias is bounded.
- `RES-001` to `RES-004` for each paper's evidence type; the papers' own internal method sections are owned by the paper repos.
- `MS-005` for where the method is reported.
