# Integrity requirements

The examined text must satisfy the evidence, quotation, terminology, and disclosure obligations below.

- **Backing:** every thesis-level claim is backed by a citation or by a result in an appended paper; the citation gate proves the citation half (`VER-003`).
- **No uncited claim:** no factual claim stands without a citation, beyond common knowledge.
- **Quotation:** no external text is used verbatim without quotation and citation.
- **Terminology:** terms follow the glossary, with one owner per term under the ownership rules (`ARG-008`).
- **Mapping:** the sub-question to paper mapping table lives in the kappa (`RQ-002`).
- **Disclosure:** AI assistance is disclosed per venue rules, and the author verifies the technical correctness of AI-assisted drafting.

**Owns:**

- Evidence, quotation, and terminology-use obligations on the text.
- Presence of the sub-question to paper mapping table in the kappa.
- Disclosure obligations attached to the written deliverable.

**Excludes:**

- `VER-003` how citations are checked and its recorded evidence.
- `VER-004` terminology and acronym consistency checks.
- `OPS-003` the glossary and acronym pipeline.
- `ARG-004` claim-to-result pointers.
- `OPS-011` format, length, and authorship.
