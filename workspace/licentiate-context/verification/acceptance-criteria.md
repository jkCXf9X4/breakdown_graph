# Acceptance criteria

The deliverable passes when all three readers pass, and each criterion tests the requirement it names.

- **Examiner:** tests the format, length, and authorship obligations (`OPS-011`, `INT-003`).
- **Opponent** (`INT-003`):
  - Probes each clause of the claim and the evidence behind it without the papers (`MS-015`).
  - Probes the text's evidence, quotation, terminology, and disclosure (`VER-006`).
- **Doctoral continuation:** tests that the chain and the contract can be inherited, so the text must state them (`MS-015`, `CLM-001`, `ARG-006`).
- **Per obligation:** `OPS-011` to `VER-006` each have at least one criterion.
- **Failing case:** a claim the papers support but the kappa leaves implicit fails all three (`INT-003`).

**Owns:**

- The acceptance criteria the deliverable must pass.
- The per-obligation mapping from criterion to requirement.

**Excludes:**

- `OPS-011`, `MS-015`, `VER-006` the obligations themselves.
- `INT-003` who the readers are and why they differ.
- `VER-001` to `VER-006` what each check proves and its recorded evidence.
- `INFO-028` review findings.
