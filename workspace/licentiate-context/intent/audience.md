# Who examines and reads the licentiate?

Three readers decide whether the licentiate stands, and each tests something different.

- **Examiner:** tests whether the papers and the kappa meet the licentiate requirements (`OPS-011`).
- **Opponent:** probes the claim and the evidence behind each clause.
  - The claim must survive a reader who has not read the papers.
- **Doctoral continuation:** reads the thesis as the base for the doctoral work.
  - The chain and the contract must be inheritable.
- **Common demand:** each reader needs the argument in the kappa, not reconstructed from the appended papers.
- **Consequence:** a claim the papers support but the kappa leaves implicit fails all three.

**Owns:**

- The three readers and what each one tests.
- The consequence that a claim left implicit in the kappa fails every reader.

**Excludes:**

- `OPS-011` for the licentiate requirements the examiner tests against.
- `MS-015` for what the kappa text must state on any reader's behalf.
- `VER-007` for the acceptance criteria that decide whether the deliverable passes.
- `MS-004` and `MS-007` for where in the kappa the argument is made available; `CLM-002` for the bounds on what may be claimed to them.
