# Glossary and acronym pipeline

Glossary text is authored in `thesis/custom/glossary.tex`; acronyms come from a generated union table.
Both are input before `\begin{document}`; the entry-point lines are `20`.

- **Authority:** `thesis/custom/glossary.tex` is the terminology authority for the kappa.
- **Entry count:** it defines 9 entries with `\newglossaryentry`; `\makeglossaries` at its line 2 arms the glossaries package.
- **Printing:** `chapters/01_terminology.tex` prints the main glossary and the acronym list.
- **Acronym wrapper:** `thesis/custom/acronyms.tex` inputs the union table, then `custom/acronyms-local`.
- **Union file:** `3rd_party/article_common_artifacts/latex/acronyms/acronyms.tex`.
- **Local additions:** `thesis/custom/acronyms-local.tex` holds kappa-only terms and local overrides; it is currently empty because the union covers every paper term.
- **Override mechanism:** local overrides use `\glsfielddef`; glossaries has no `\renewacronym`.
- **Generator:** `3rd_party/article_common_artifacts/latex/acronyms/build_union.py` scans every `**/*.tex` under each root for `\newacronym` definitions.
- **Root order:** `papers/Simulation_and_models`, `papers/model_domain_classification`, `papers/automation_complex_vv_workflows`, `papers/2026_purpose_driven_modelling`; the generated header also lists `thesis/custom` under the label `kappa`.
- **Conflict resolution:** later roots win; `--prefer` names an explicit winner.
- **Output:** `--out-dir` is required and receives `acronyms.tex` and `conflicts.md`; conflicting forms exit 2 unless `--allow-conflicts` is passed.
- **Default excludes:** the scan skips `3rd_party`, `node_modules`, `build`, and `dist`.
- **Accepted conflicts:** `3rd_party/article_common_artifacts/latex/acronyms/resolutions.yml` records them as `key: SHORT|LONG`; it holds one entry, `se: SE|Simulation Engine`.
- **Accepted keys:** a resolved key stays visible in `conflicts.md` and stops failing the run.
- **Generated state:** the union defines 125 keys; a regeneration at the current pins reports 3 unresolved conflicts, two form (`hla`, `liu`) and one option (`opscon`), and the committed `conflicts.md` is stale, still reporting none.
- **Generated files:** `acronyms.tex` and `conflicts.md` are generated; never hand-edit them.

**Owns:**

- Where glossary and acronym text is authored, generated, and accepted.
- The generated-state counts of the union table and its conflict file.

**Excludes:**

- Terminology ownership and reserved meanings: `106`.
- Regeneration routine and triggers: `104`.
- Terminology consistency gate: `103`.
- The LaTeX input wiring: `20`.
- Terminology obligations on the text: `42`.
