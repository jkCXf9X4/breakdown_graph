# How are the survey and the case bounded?

The validity rules for the survey and the in-repo case bound what the kappa may claim from them.

- **Survey reporting rule:** the survey is reported as exploratory, with instrument, sampling frame and response rate stated so a reader can judge generalizability; triangulation with independent experience reports is the validity strategy, because a representative sampling frame often cannot be established.
- **Single-case rule:** the case study answers three evaluation questions about one aerospace case in two versions and is used only for what a single case can show: that the mechanism operates end to end on a realistic artifact set.
- **Single-case limits:** it is not evidence of effort reduction, defect reduction, or transferability; transferability of the workflow is argued from its construction, not demonstrated.
- **Generality rule:** a model's validation domain is not its application domain, so extrapolation is stated rather than assumed.
- **Case method:** the in-repo case is worked once and read by inspection; each clause is recovered from a committed artifact, and the case claims no empirical result (`74`).
- **Claim:** the rules bound the evidence claims (`49`, `3`).
