# Acceptance criteria

The deliverable passes when all three readers pass, and each criterion tests the requirement it names.

- **Examiner:** tests the format, length, and authorship obligations (`29`, `27`).
- **Opponent** (`27`):
  - Probes each clause of the claim and the evidence behind it without the papers (`13`).
  - Probes the text's evidence, quotation, terminology, and disclosure (`42`).
- **Doctoral continuation:** tests that the chain and the contract can be inherited, so the text must state them (`13`, `49`, `94`).
- **Per obligation:** `29` to `42` each have at least one criterion.
- **Failing case:** a claim the papers support but the kappa leaves implicit fails all three (`27`).

**Owns:**

- The acceptance criteria the deliverable must pass.
- The per-obligation mapping from criterion to requirement.

**Excludes:**

- `29`, `13`, `42` the obligations themselves.
- `27` who the readers are and why they differ.
- `76` to `42` what each check proves and its recorded evidence.
- `128` review findings.
