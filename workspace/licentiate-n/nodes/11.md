# Retypeset Paper 1 as LaTeX

Paper I's version of record is the published Springer article, which the kappa imports retypeset through a kappa adapter.
**Evidence:**

The facsimile carried its own layout, fonts, page geometry, and reference list; the body was a published version of record, so every change to it had to be deliberate, and absorbing it as LaTeX lets it share the thesis layout and cross-reference machinery.

- 199 edits were ported from the published version of record onto the source; `main` sits at `56ff392` with tag `published`, and the re-diff against the published text left 82 residual hunks, all artifact-classified (layout, hyphenation, line breaks).
- The McGraw Hill source keeps its published year 2011 in the body while the `.bib` carries 1936.
- The "caped" caption typo is kept, since it sits in both the published text and the source; the published "Fig.1" typo was not ported.
- Both builds pass: the standalone paper on `main` and `kappa`, and the thesis at the recorded pins (exit 0, 191 pages, 0 undefined citations, 0 named undefined references, no `??` in the rendered PDF).
- Paper I's span runs 36 pages at pages 69-104, where the facsimile occupied 27.
**Outcome:**

Paper I enters the appendix as LaTeX through `\includearticletex{paper1_simulation_and_models}` (`20`).
The adapter lives at `papers/Simulation_and_models/kappa/paper.tex`, one commit past `published` on branch `kappa` (`734ed69`).
Eight sections demote to subsection and 22 to subsubsection; labels and refs carry the `p1:` prefix (21 labels, 56 refs), and the float set is 9 figures (3 wide) and 3 tables (2 wide), no listings.
`thesis/build.py` merges Paper 1's `sn-bibliography.bib` first, so first source wins, and the cover page keeps the `\fullcite` entry from `thesis/papers.bib`.
The parent gitlink still pins `cb91198`; the bump to `734ed69` waits on the parent commit.
The retypesetting is a policy change; the policy also omits Paper I's paper-local Terminology section, so the book's Terminology chapter is the single authority.
**Reference alignment:**

All Andersson citations across Papers 1, 2, 3 and 6 use the library keys `anderssonSaabAeronauticsHandbook2012` and `anderssonVariabilityCustomizationSimulator2012`, with byte-identical bodies in all four; the legacy keys `andersson_saab_2012`, `andersson_variability_2012`, `Andersson2012`, `Andersson2012a` and `Andersson2012A` are gone.
All Potts citations use the canonical key `pottsSoftwareengineeringResearchRevisited1993`; `Potts1993` is gone from every `.bib` and from the merged union.
Papers 1, 2 and 3 carry the library body — one journal field, year 1993, number 5, pages 19--28, bare DOI — and, with Paper 6 (`09786e4`), the version-of-record note that restores `Backup Publisher`, `ISBN`, `Issue 1427` and `Series` to the printed entry.
The library body at `5b5decf` carries the DiVA URN `https://urn.kb.se/resolve?urn=urn:nbn:se:liu:diva-73572`, no WETICE DOI, one institution, and exported `number` and `note`.
Paper 6 keeps a biblatex-shaped body (`date`, `journaltitle`, `doi = {doi: ...}`), so the merge still reports one body difference there; Paper 1 owns the key, so the printed entry is the library body.
The merge reports 12 warnings (11 pre-existing Paper 2-5 duplicates plus the Potts body difference), a merged union of 1405 entries, and a printed list of 263.
The parent pins the library at `98ca35e`; the pushed repair `5b5decf` stays unpinned and renders the eight kappa entries byte-identically.
