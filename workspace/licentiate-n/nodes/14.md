# Requirement coverage

Every `REQ-` obligation is proved by one check, or it is unproven and this leaf says so.

- `29` format, length, authorship — `51` proves length and assembly; the abstract word count, the Swedish summary, the delimitations and the author contributions have no check (4 unproven).
- `13` what the kappa must state — `76` proves that each contribution names a paper result; the seven statements other than the delimitation have no check (7 unproven).
- `42` integrity — `17` proves backing and `103` proves terminology; no-uncited-claim, quotation, the mapping table's presence and AI disclosure have no check (4 unproven).
- `28` acceptance criteria — unproven (1): no check runs a reader pass, and `128` records findings rather than a pass.
- Rule: a requirement with no check is a promise nobody can fail; a reviewer finds it before the gate does.
- Unproven obligations: 16 — 4 in `29`, 7 in `13`, 4 in `42`, 1 in `28`; the delimitation is counted once, under `29`.
- `16` carries the decision on each unproven obligation: a check, or review-only by explicit choice.

**Owns:**

- The requirement-to-check map: which leaf proves which `REQ-` obligation.
- The unproven list and its count.
- The rule that an unproven obligation is a promise nobody can fail.

**Excludes:**

- The obligations themselves (`29` to `28`).
- What each check proves in detail, and its evidence (`51`, `17`, `103`, `76`).
- The review findings that stand in for a missing check (`128`).
- The plan that would close the gap (`16`).
