# What does the licentiate cover?

The licentiate covers four appended papers and a kappa that connects them.

- **Paper set:** four papers, all appended; each answers one sub-question (`41`).
- **Examined object:** the deliverable is one PDF: a kappa, four appended papers, and one in-repo case appendix; Paper I is a journal paper and Papers II–IV conference papers.
- **Interface scope:** FMI and SSP bound the interfaces the thesis examines (`102`).
- **Synthesis:** the kappa connects the papers by making their implicit dependencies explicit (`84`).
- **Bounded case:** Paper IV carries one architecture-to-system-definition handover with guarded reverse synchronization (`4`).
- **Beyond the paper set:** one in-repo case carries the demonstration (`74`).
- **Setting:** large-scale CPS development; a single tool and a single model fall outside (`34`).
- **Evidence bound:** no new empirical work; evidence stops at the papers and the in-repo case (`3`).
- **Boundary:** execution and evaluation are delimitations outside the objective.

**Owns:**

- The inclusion decision itself: which papers are appended, which standards bound the interfaces, and what lies outside.

**Excludes:**

- `6` for how the examined object is presented as one PDF; `119` for how kappa and papers are ordered.
- `44` for the Included Papers list as a deliverable contract.
- `43` to `4` for paper identity, type, roles, and evidence bound; `35` for the chain roles.
- `34` and `41` for the question wording and its decomposition into sub-questions.
