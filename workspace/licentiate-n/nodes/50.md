# How is each claim mapped to a paper result?

This node holds the claim-to-evidence index: which kappa claim is tied to which appended-paper result, and with what bound.

- **Pointer convention:** a pointer is a `\Cref` to a namespaced label in the appendix, `p1:`, `p2:`, `p3:`, or `p6:` as the key stands, never just the paper; it names a section, figure, or table, and where the paper has no label, the section title.
- **Pointer robustness:** because the label is namespaced and resolved by the build, a pointer to a result that disappears fails the build instead of failing silently.
- **Why line:** a pointer adds one line stating why that result supports the claim.
- **Synthesis pointer:** the synthesis claim points to Papers I to IV, not to a single figure; the thesis claim (`49`) points to the four-paper set and to `tab:contract-applied` on the in-repo case (`74`).
- **Current map:** contributions cite, in order, Paper I survey figures, Paper II OD/DoV and verification evidence, Paper III workflow schema and provenance, Paper IV guarded round-trip and case study; the bounds are reported practice, proof of concept, one framework, and one aerospace case.
- **Conclusions:** the four conclusions each name one element of the chain and its pointer: the three clauses and the premise (`35`).
- **Handovers:** the three handovers and the premise are paired in `tab:taxonomy`, each handover with an appended paper (`89`).
- **Consequence status:** locatable is enumerable at the named handovers, demonstrated at three gaps, and the count is its own; repairable and traceable are demonstrated once, on the in-repo case, where all three clauses are recoverable from artifacts; cost is asserted and bounded, with no measured figure (`3`).

**Owns:**

- The evidence pointer convention.
- The current claim count, per-consequence status, evidence cells and gap cells.

**Excludes:**

- `76` for the traceability acceptance criterion and its limits.
- `84` and `89` for the chain stages and handovers; `35` for the paper roles.
- `43` to `74` for what each paper and the case establish; the results inside each paper are owned by the paper repos.
- `13` for the obligation this map serves; `42` for the mapping table's presence; `14` for the coverage map.
