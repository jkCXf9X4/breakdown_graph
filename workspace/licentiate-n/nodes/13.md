# Text obligations

The kappa must state every element below in its own text, so a reader who has not read the papers never has to reconstruct one.

- **Claim:** stated in the wording fixed for it (`49`).
- **Failure modes:** a handover either carries the information the next step needs, corrupts it, or loses it (`89`).
- **Strength:** the claim is stated as weaker than a weakest-link property and as the thesis's own construct (`70`).
- **Refutation:** both refutation conditions are stated (`70`).
- **Refusals:** the four refusals are stated, each with its bound (`3`).
- **Delimitation:** the text states that the contract has three clauses (`94`) and that execution and evaluation lie outside the objective (`2`).
- **Contract:** the three-clause contract, the per-decision scope, the operating conditions travelling with the claim, and the cost must all be stated in the text (`94`).
- **Open cost:** the text carries the caveat that no paper measures the cost of a wrong judgement (`33`).

**Owns:**

- The obligations on the kappa text that derive from the intent layer.
- Which intent statements must appear in the text.

**Excludes:**

- `49`, `3`, `83`, `70`, `94`, `33` hold the statements themselves; `2` and `89` hold the delimitation and the failure modes.
- `107` to `119` where each statement is printed.
- `50` how claims are traced to results.
- `28` whether the stated text passes.
