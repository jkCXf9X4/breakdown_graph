# Licentiate requirements

The examined text must satisfy the faculty format, length, and authorship obligations below.

- **On the text:** these are obligations on the written deliverable, not on the toolchain that produces it.
- **Abstract:** 250–300 words; no check enforces the count (`14`).
- **Swedish summary:** a popular-science summary of about one page that is a faithful equivalent of the abstract.
- **Delimitations:** stated explicitly as boundaries of the work, not left as omissions.
- **Author contributions:** stated per appended paper.
- **Tester:** the examiner tests compliance with these obligations (`27`).

**Owns:**

- Faculty format, length, and authorship obligations on the kappa.
- Presence of the abstract, the Swedish summary, the delimitations, and the author contributions.

**Excludes:**

- `13` the statements the argument must carry.
- `42` evidence, quotation, terminology, and disclosure.
- `28` the acceptance criteria.
- `107` to `119` chapter and front-matter order.
- `108` glossary and acronym pipeline.
- `122` the rewrite of the Swedish summary itself.
