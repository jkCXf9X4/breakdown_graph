# Figure and table inventory

The kappa carries ten floats of its own: two figures and eight tables, spread over the numbered chapters.
Chapter order and float placement are the per-chapter contracts `107` to `119`.

**Figures:**
- `fig:development-chain` renders `design_workflow.pdf` in the Introduction (`115`).
- `fig:credibility-chain` is an inline TikZ drawing; its chapter and section are `75`, the reading is `84`.
- It lays five boxes in one row — definition, intended use, evidence, execution as a boundary, evaluation as a cited check — with a dashed premise frame above the row, a double-headed cost arrow below it, and a result arrow closing the row.
- Its caption calls the figure a synthesis model and not a process claim, because a change anywhere reopens the clauses downstream of it.

**Tables:**
- `tab:credibility_scales` lives in `thesis/tables/credibility_scales.tex` and the Background includes it (`99`).
- `tab:framework-coverage` is written inline; where it sits and what else the chapter carries are `99`.
- `tab:subquestion-paper-map` is the Research Questions and Contributions chapter's sub-question-to-paper mapping table (`116`), written inline at `thesis/chapters/04_research_questions_and_contributions.tex:86`.
- `tab:evidence-base` and `tab:artifacts` sit in the Methods chapter (`93`).
- `tab:contract-applied`, `tab:paper-roles`, and `tab:taxonomy` sit in the Synthesis chapter (`75`).
- The Summary, Conclusions and Author Contributions chapters carry no floats (`5`, `46`, `117`).

**Totals:**
- Ten kappa floats: two figures, eight tables, ten labelled and none unlabelled.
- Counted from `\begin{figure}` and `\begin{table}` in `thesis/chapters/*.tex` plus `thesis/tables/`.
- Every float is reachable by `\ref`; the Research Questions and Contributions chapter's mapping table gained `tab:subquestion-paper-map` after an earlier unlabelled pass.
- Appended-paper floats are outside this inventory; the kappa's own floats are the unit of count.

- The form of each float: inline or included source, internal layout, and any caption caveat.
- The kappa float count.

**Excludes:**

- Chapter order and which section carries which float (`107` to `119`).
- The pipelines and build steps that produce figure and table sources (`20`, `24`).
- Whether an asset renders correctly or is correct in content (`51`, `17`, `103`, `50`).
