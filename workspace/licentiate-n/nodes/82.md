- **`77` derived or stipulated?** **Stipulated.** `03:80` already says "a requirement of the contract, not an existing standard" and the audit graded it DEFINITIONAL; deriving it would make the contract a result of six papers written before it existed.
- **`78` checklist, score, or diagnostic?** **Diagnostic.** The only option that keeps `07:66-99` (the one original instrument, R-191) and the glossary's "satisfied or not satisfied" both true, and it is what `07:174` already claims.
- **`79` run the contract on Paper VI, and is 2/4 publishable?** **Run it.** A rubric returning 2/4 on every input is a description, and the examiner will ask; if Paper VI also returns 2/4, that is a finding, not a failure.
- **`87` are SQ1 and SQ5 premise and instrument rather than parts?** **Reclassify.** The "parts" claim becomes true for four of six and the primary question is untouched; `84` already calls step 1 the premise that "carries no clause".
