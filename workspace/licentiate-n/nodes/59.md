## The two structural facts behind most of these

`03_background.tex:41-80` is one 952-word section doing three jobs in the wrong order
(review, then novelty claim, then definition), so the definitional core of the thesis sits
buried in a subsection of a literature review. And the six-row paper-to-chain-step mapping
is printed three times (`04:97-102`, `06:6-31`, `07:103-125`), which is why the enumerations
drift apart.
