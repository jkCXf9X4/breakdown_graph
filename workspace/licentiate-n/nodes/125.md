# Strengthen the library's text-adequacy criterion

The library audit found 60 of 61 cited keys ADEQUATE and one SUSPECT: `oberkampfVerificationValidationScientific2010`.
Its `text.md` is 18,791 characters over 13 pages — a ResearchGate cover, blurb, copyright page, dedication, table of contents to p. 762 (the Index), list of figures, preface (pp. xi-xiv) and acknowledgements — extracted with `pdftotext -layout`; no chapter body is there, and the book is roughly 800 pages (Parts I-V).
The book is closed access (CUP; OpenAlex `oa_status` closed; Unpaywall `is_oa` false), so no legitimate copy can be intaked.
**Evidence:**

The library's adequacy criterion is "does `text.md` exist and extract cleanly", and this document satisfies it while being unquotable for anything past the preface: a future chapter-level claim would be written against a text that does not contain it, and no existing check would catch it.
The three flags measure the text layer's *quality*, not its *scope*, so a clean extraction of the wrong pages passes all of them; the criterion that catches this defect class is not a flag.

- `text.md` is 18,791 characters, 13 pages, front matter only; `content.md` (21,145 characters, `pymupdf4llm`) has the same extent, and its only headings are the title, `Contents`, `Preface` and `Acknowledgments`.
- The PDF manifest pins a 191,119-byte blob. Over the 725 pages its own table of contents declares, that is 264 bytes per page; every other cited key with a declared extent is at 3,364 or above.
- Bytes per *extracted* page does not separate it: at 14,701 it ranks sixth of 59 cited keys, mid-pack, because front matter is dense text. The extent, not the density, is the signal.
- `text_poor` false, `ocr_needed` false, `text_order_score` 0.0, `verified_source` `crossref:doi`; `refs lint --text-quality` reports ok and its 2026-11-01 report does not name this key.
- The cited sentence is in the Preface, p. xi, so the single existing citation is sound.
- Run over all 61 cited keys, the criterion fails this key alone: extent 13 of 725 pages (coverage 0.018) and zero body headings.
- Review record `128`; audit: `.dynamic-harness/261101_082500_e339/review/library-audit.md`, the single SUSPECT entry.
**Acceptance:**

- The limitation is recorded against `oberkampfVerificationValidationScientific2010` in the library record, so no chapter-level claim is made against it without a note.
- The adequacy criterion requires body coverage: `text_pages` is checked against the document's real extent, and at least one body section heading must be present in `text.md` and in `content.md`.
- The criterion is written where the intake and lint tooling reads it, and a document of this shape fails it.
- The leaf records that `text_poor`, `ocr_needed` and `text_order_score` cannot detect this defect class, and that PDF bytes per page plus a body-heading check can.
- `23`'s acceptance is tightened to the same criterion, or `23` points at this leaf for it.
- The closed-access status and the reason no copy can be intaked are recorded, so the gap is not re-opened as an intake task.
**Criterion in force:**

The criterion itself — extent against the document's declared page count, and at least one non-front-matter heading in `content.md` — is stated verbatim in `23`'s acceptance.
The checks run by hand: neither `refs lint --text-quality` (order and CJK only) nor `pb` reads extent or headings, and adding them to the tool is not this leaf's scope.
**Where the limitation is recorded:**

The library records per-document caveats in `info.yaml`'s `note:` field, which 19 records already use for exactly this kind of statement; the note is not yet committed there (`3rd_party/references`, submodule at `5b5decf`). Until it lands, the limitation stands in this leaf and in `62`'s `R-211` row, where a chapter-level claim against this key would be checked. The note to add is:

> `note: Front matter only. text.md holds the cover, table of contents to p. 762 (the Index), preface (pp. xi-xiv) and acknowledgements; the book body (Parts I-V, 800+ pp) is absent. The one kappa citation is grounded in the Preface at p. xi. No chapter-level claim can be checked against this record. Closed access (CUP), so no copy can be intaked.`
**Out of scope:**

- Intaking the book, which the licence forbids.
- The other 60 keys, which the audit found adequate.
- The 206 DOI-less records reported by `refs lint`.
