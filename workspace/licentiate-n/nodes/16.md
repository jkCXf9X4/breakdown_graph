# Gate the text obligations

- **Priority:** high — it is the only requirement set with no check at all, and its failure mode appears at the defence rather than at the gate.

No check in Verification tests whether the kappa states what `13` obliges it to state.
`76` proves only that each contribution names a paper result, which is the pointing, not the statement; the remaining seven statements — claim wording, strength, refutation, refusals, per-decision scope and cost, the unmeasured-cost caveat and the implicit-claim consequence — are enforced by nothing but a reader.
**Evidence:**

An obligation with no check is a promise nobody can fail, so the defect that matters most to the opponent — the thesis claim printed in weaker or stronger words than the papers support, or a refusal stated without its bound — is discovered at the defence rather than at the gate.
`14` records 16 unproven obligations, so this is not one leaf's oversight: no leaf was ever written for the statements, only for the mechanisms around them.

- `14` records `13` as "`76` proves that each contribution names a paper result; the seven statements other than the delimitation have no check (7 unproven)".
- `14` records `28` as "unproven (1): no check runs a reader pass, and `128` records findings rather than a pass", so `28` has no check at all; `13` is the unproven requirement this leaf names.
**Acceptance:**

- Each unproven obligation in `14` either gains a check with a criterion and recorded evidence under `verification/`, or is recorded as accepted review-only with the reason and who reads it.
- The decision for every unproven obligation is written down and `14` restated, so the count falls to the accepted review-only set and no obligation stays unknown.
- `13` gains a presence check: for each of the seven statements, a recorded location in the kappa or an open change to write it, sufficient that a reader can find the statement without reading the papers.
**Out of scope:**

- Writing the missing statements themselves, which is an edit to the kappa chapters; a second rater or a reader study behind `28`.
- The citation, terminology and build gates (`51`, `17`, `103`).

- The obligations themselves (`29` to `28`).
- The coverage map this leaf closes (`14`).
- What each existing check proves (`51`, `17`, `103`, `76`, `128`).
