## Does the thesis hold up?

**Yes, with three repairs.** The argument is sound and the evidence base is real: the six
papers exist, the chain from definition to evaluation is genuinely covered, and the
retrospective application at `07:66-99` is an original instrument no paper contains.

What does not hold is the **presentation of the central construct**: the contract is the
thesis's own construction and the book never says so. It is used at `03:53` before it is stated
at `03:80`, stated as a definition rather than derived, then scored at `07:79` and `07:98` in a
chapter that denies it is a score and a glossary that says it "is satisfied or not satisfied
rather than scored" — where an examiner stops.

The three must-fix defects are rows 1–4 below: introduce the contract as an argument step
(R-001–R-003), narrow the two false universals (R-005, R-006), remove the provenance claim
(R-004: `provenance` has zero hits in Papers III and VI). What is strong is `62`.
