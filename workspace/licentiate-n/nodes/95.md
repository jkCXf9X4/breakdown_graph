# What does the SSP standard itself establish?

The SSP standard specifies system composition and parameterization, and it defines what a composition artifact must carry.

- **Composition:** SSP bundles the signal flow and parameterization of each model, and variants of a structure as one SSP.
- **Unbound:** a parameter such as `fmi_CommunicationStepSize` may be declared and left unbound; nothing requires it to be set.
- **Absence:** the standard does not define execution-specific settings or intended use as required fields.
- **Source:** `modelicaassociationSystemStructureParameterization2019`, with the intended-use reading from `rosenlundObjectivelyDefinedIntended2024`; both verified in the pinned library's extracted text.
- **Verification:** the absence claims are readings of the spec's own text; per-sentence verdicts land under `111`.
