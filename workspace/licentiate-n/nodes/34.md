# Primary research question

The primary research question is:

> How can standardized, machine-interoperable M&S metadata make the credibility of a simulation result explicit, auditable and traceable during development of large-scale cyber-physical systems?

- **Orientation:** a mechanism question, because the descriptive variant — how much credibility is lost — is not answerable by this evidence base.
- **Answer:** credibility for a decision belongs to the chain that produces the result, not to the models that enter it (`49`).
- **M&S metadata:** the machine-interoperable description of what must be known about a model, a composed system, or the evidence for it.
- **Kinds:** model and system definitions (`102`), plus verification evidence as recorded V&V outcomes and the links stating what each establishes (`94`).
- **Standardized and machine-interoperable:** fixed by a published, versioned specification outside any single tool, so independent implementations produce and consume it and tooling checks it (`108`).
- **Explicit, auditable, traceable:** the contract's three clauses carried as artifacts, not raised numerical accuracy (`94`).
- **Traceable:** two objects, the definition and the evidence, in both directions (`106`).
- **Setting:** large-scale CPS development, not a single tool and a single model (`2`).

**Owns:**

- Verbatim wording of the primary research question.
- The three metadata kinds the question works with: model definition, system definition, verification evidence.
- Mechanism orientation of the question, and the rejection of a descriptive variant.

**Excludes:**

- The four sub-questions and their paper mapping (`41`).
- The thesis claim and its calibrated strength (`49`).
- Problem statement and its three gaps (`1`).
- Credibility stance itself (`94`).
- Definition layer as an architectural device (`102`).
- Scope limits of the study (`2`).
