# How are findings graded and where do they route?

A finding names a defect and a fix, then leaves this layer; only the criteria stay here.

- **BLOCKER:** the central claim is unsupported, self-contradictory, or unreadable as written; an examiner stops here.
- **MAJOR:** a claim exceeds its evidence, a structural promise is broken, or a section does not do its job.
- **MINOR:** local inaccuracy, terminology drift, redundancy.
- **NOTE:** verified-good (protect it) or cosmetic.
- **Findings are transient:** the 2026-11 run's 212 rows are history in `32`, not state; a new run writes its rows to an investigation the same way.
- **Route:** a defect is fixed in the owning leaf of the layer where the claim lives; a fix that needs a project decision is an IMP in evolution; a committed choice is a decision record. The fix lands in the owning layer, never in review/.
- **Key history:** the severity key came from `56`; the blockers it graded are `57`.
