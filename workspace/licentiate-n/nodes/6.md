# How is the examined object presented?

The examined object ships as one PDF: the kappa, four appended papers, and the case appendix, composed into one text.

- **One text:** the kappa and Papers I–IV ship as one PDF, the papers after the kappa (`119`).
- **Form:** all four papers ship retypeset in the thesis layout; Paper I aligns to the published version of record.
- **Case appendix:** the book prints one case appendix after the paper supplements (`119`).

**Owns:**

- How the examined object is presented: one PDF, the paper ordering, and the retypeset form of each appended paper.
- That the shipped book includes the case appendix.

**Excludes:**

- `2` what the examined object is made of: the kappa, the paper set, and the paper types.
- `43` to `4` paper identity, status, objectives, roles, and evidence bounds.
- `38` the paper house style the retypeset bodies share, and the adapter contract.
- `24` build and submodule pins.
- `22` bibliography rendering and the single printed list.
- `107` to `119` chapter order, appendix placement, and render order.
- `44` the Included Papers list.
- `26` venue, DOI, and publication metadata upkeep.
