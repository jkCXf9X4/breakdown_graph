# What does the Synthesis and Discussion chapter deliver?

The synthesis is where the kappa earns the thesis-level claim: it applies the contract, reconciles the taxonomy, and states what the papers contribute.

- **Purpose:** make the kappa's own argument rather than re-summarizing the papers; that is what supports `49` (`84`).
- **First half:** The argument in brief; Credibility as a contract; Applying the contract to the in-repo case; What the papers contribute to the contract; Standardized interfaces as enablers and constraints.
- **Second half:** One taxonomy, three clauses; Where credibility breaks; Execution and evaluation as scope limits; Implications and scope limits.
- **Figure:** `fig:credibility-chain`, an inline TikZ drawing under Credibility as a contract, reads the Paper I premise as a frame above three stages (`84`, `85`).
- **Tables:** `tab:contract-applied` applies the contract to the in-repo case with the completeness indicator; `tab:taxonomy` is the only place gaps, clauses, kinds and papers align; What the papers contribute to the contract carries one table in chain order IV, I, II, III, marking the Paper I row as the premise.
- **Register:** the contract must be stated as derived or as the thesis's own construction, and never scored where the book denies it is a score (`77`, `78`).
