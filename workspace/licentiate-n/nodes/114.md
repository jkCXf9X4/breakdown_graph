# What does reproducibility mean in the simulation literature?

The kappa's determinism definition operationalizes an established reproducibility concept.

- **Repeatability:** the ability to re-execute the same simulation history, the repeatability level of reproducibility (`dalleReproducibilityTraceabilitySimulations2012`).
- **Non-determinism:** a non-deterministic interface destroys reproducibility whether or not the models are deterministic (`pedersenCoSimulationCyberPhysicalSystem2018`).

- **Serves:** the operational determinism definition (`98`) and its report in the methods chapter (`93`).
